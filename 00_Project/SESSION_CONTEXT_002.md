---

# 1. Project Objective

The objective is **not** simply to rewrite an existing CV.

The objective is to build a complete **Executive Career Portfolio**, from which multiple professional assets can later be generated, including:

- Executive CV
- ATS-Optimized CV
- LinkedIn Profile
- Executive Biography
- Cover Letters
- Interview STAR Stories
- Executive Portfolio
- Professional Knowledge Repository

The Executive CV is considered one deliverable generated from this broader knowledge base.

---

# 2. Strategic Principles

The following principles have been adopted for the entire project.

## 2.1 Engineering Approach

The project is developed exactly as an engineering project.

Architecture is designed first.

Deliverables are generated afterwards.

---

## 2.2 Architecture before Documents

Documents are not independent.

Each document defines a reusable layer of professional knowledge.

---

## 2.3 Single Source of Truth

Professional experience is documented only once.

Every future deliverable (CV, LinkedIn, Interview answers, etc.) will reuse the same knowledge base.

---

## 2.4 Modular Documentation

Large documents are avoided.

Knowledge is organized into reusable modules.

---

# 3. Language Strategy

The working language for architecture documents is English because:

- ATS systems perform better.
- AI semantic analysis is stronger.
- Executive terminology is more standardized.

The final Executive CV for the Peruvian market will be produced in Spanish.

Therefore:

Architecture → English

Final CV → Spanish

# 5. Career Achievement Methodology

Original idea:

One document containing all achievements.

Current decision:

Rejected.

Instead, achievements are classified into three levels.

---

## Level 1

Achievement Cards

Simple reusable achievements.

Purpose:

Generate CV bullets.

Estimated quantity:

Approximately 50.

---

## Level 2

Executive Cases

Projects requiring deeper explanation.

Estimated quantity:

Approximately 10.

---

## Level 3

Signature Executive Cases

Career-defining projects.

Complete engineering case studies.

Estimated quantity:

3–5.

# 9. New Architectural Discovery

During CASE-001 and CASE-002 analysis an important pattern was identified.

The user's professional value is not primarily the achievements themselves.

The differentiator is the engineering methodology repeatedly used to solve industrial problems.

Observed pattern:

Observe

↓

Understand entire system

↓

Challenge accepted assumptions

↓

Identify hidden constraints

↓

Generate engineering hypothesis

↓

Validate technically

↓

Align stakeholders

↓

Implement

↓

Standardize

↓

Transfer knowledge

This pattern appears consistently across independent projects.
