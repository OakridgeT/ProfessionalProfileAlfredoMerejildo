# 1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is **NOT** simply to update an existing Curriculum Vitae.

The objective is to design a complete **Executive Career Portfolio**, equivalent to the documentation used in a professional engineering project.

The CV will become one document inside a much larger professional positioning strategy.

The final objective is to position Alfredo Merejildo as a strong candidate for executive positions such as:

* Gerente de Manufactura
* Gerente de Operaciones
* Jefe de Planta
* Gerente Industrial
* Gerente de Excelencia Operacional
* Gerente de Transformación Digital Industrial

---

# 2. Career Strategy

# 3. Fundamental Positioning

A critical strategic decision was made.

The candidate will NOT be positioned primarily as:

* Mechanical Engineer
* Production Engineer
* Maintenance Engineer

Instead, the professional positioning will be:

> Gerente de Manufactura y Operaciones especializado en transformar operaciones industriales mediante liderazgo, mejora continua e innovación tecnológica.

This positioning will guide every future document.

# 4. Executive Value Proposition

The entire Career Portfolio will communicate one central message:

> Alfredo transforms industrial operations by increasing productivity, reducing operational costs, developing high-performance teams and integrating Industry 4.0 technologies into manufacturing environments.

The CV must not describe activities.

# 6. Industry 4.0 Positioning

One of the most important strategic decisions concerns the period after 2023.

The software, electronics and AI work developed through the BNQTA project will NOT be presented as a career change into software development.

Instead, it will be positioned as:

> An executive initiative focused on Industrial Digital Transformation.

The narrative will communicate that after nearly two decades leading manufacturing operations, Alfredo identified an opportunity to solve industrial problems through Artificial Intelligence, Industrial IoT and predictive analytics.

This period becomes an evolution of executive capabilities instead of an employment gap.

# 8. Core Competencies Strategy

The traditional "Technical Skills" section will be replaced by a competency architecture organized into executive capability groups.

Planned competency groups include:

* Manufacturing Operations
* Continuous Improvement
* Engineering & Asset Management
* Business & Financial Management
* Leadership
* Digital Manufacturing & Industry 4.0

This organization is intended to improve ATS matching while reinforcing executive positioning.

---

# 9. Experience Rewriting Methodology

A major strategic decision was taken.

Professional experience will NOT be rewritten directly into CV format.

Instead, each company will first be documented using an extended professional analysis.

Each company profile will include:

* Business Context
* Strategic Responsibility
* Main Challenges
* Decisions Taken
* Results Achieved
* Demonstrated Competencies
* ATS Keywords
* Reusable Career Achievements

Only after completing this analysis will the condensed CV version be written.

---

# 10. Career Achievements Repository

Rather than collecting only the achievements that appear in the CV, a complete repository of approximately 50–70 measurable achievements will be created.

These achievements will later be reused for:

* Executive CV
* LinkedIn
* Executive interviews
* Cover letters
* Executive biographies

The repository becomes the professional knowledge base of the Career Portfolio.

---

# 11. Design Philosophy

A fundamental rule was established during this session.

Every sentence written throughout the Career Portfolio must satisfy at least one of the following objectives:

* Improve ATS positioning.
* Strengthen executive branding.
* Differentiate the candidate.
* Reinforce the professional narrative.
* Prepare future interview discussions.

No text should remain merely because it sounds attractive.

Every paragraph must have a strategic purpose.

---

# 12. Language Strategy

Although many multinational companies accept English CVs, the Executive Career Portfolio will be developed primarily in Spanish.

Reasons:

* Primary target market is Peru and Latin America.
* Initial recruiters are usually Spanish-speaking.
* Manufacturing executive positions are commonly recruited in Spanish.
* International terminology (Lean Manufacturing, OEE, TPM, CAPEX, IIoT, Industry 4.0, etc.) will be preserved where appropriate.

An English version may be developed later from the Spanish master document.
