# Executive Summary

After more than two decades leading manufacturing operations, industrial engineering projects and process optimization initiatives, I reached a recurring conclusion:

Most industrial monitoring systems observe production outputs, but very few understand the functional behavior of the manufacturing process itself.

This realization became the starting point of BNQTA (Industrial Functional Intelligence Platform), a long-term technology initiative created to develop a new generation of industrial monitoring systems capable of interpreting machine behavior rather than simply counting production results.

More than a software or hardware project, BNQTA represents the convergence of my experience in manufacturing, process engineering, industrial analytics, systems thinking and executive leadership into a single technological platform.

The project has also represented a profound professional transformation, requiring the acquisition of competencies far beyond traditional manufacturing management, including embedded electronics, firmware development, IoT architectures, cloud platforms, industrial databases, software engineering, artificial intelligence and technology entrepreneurship.

---

# 1. Professional Background

Throughout my professional career I consistently worked on solving complex industrial problems.

Whether improving manufacturing processes, redesigning production systems, reducing operating costs or developing predictive analytical models, my approach remained essentially the same:

- Understand the complete system.
- Identify the true operational constraint.
- Build a conceptual model.
- Design an integrated solution.

Over time I began noticing a recurring limitation across many manufacturing companies.

Managers were making strategic decisions using information that represented production results rather than the actual behavior of the production process.

Machines continuously generated valuable operational information, yet most industrial monitoring systems ignored it.

---

# 2. The Origin of BNQTA

The idea originated during my time at Alicorp.

The company implemented a commercial Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) monitoring system capable of collecting production information in real time.

Although technically successful, I identified an important limitation.

The system functioned primarily as a production counter.

It measured outputs, downtime and production indicators, but captured very little information about the physical behavior of the manufacturing process itself.

This observation raised a question that would eventually define the direction of the project:

**What if industrial systems could understand how a process behaves instead of simply recording what it produces?**

That question became the conceptual foundation of BNQTA.

---

# 3. From Monitoring to Industrial Intelligence

The original objective was relatively modest.

Develop a monitoring platform capable of overcoming the limitations observed in conventional OEE systems.

However, as the project evolved, a much broader opportunity emerged.

Instead of measuring production events, the platform could analyze electrical, mechanical and operational signatures to understand the functional state of industrial equipment.

This shift transformed BNQTA from a monitoring system into an Industrial Functional Intelligence Platform.

Its purpose is no longer limited to reporting what happened.

Its purpose is to explain why it happened and to anticipate what is likely to happen next.

---

# 5. Engineering Philosophy

Throughout the project one idea became increasingly clear.

Industrial competitiveness depends on understanding processes, not simply measuring production.

Many manufacturing companies operate below their true potential because existing control systems were designed decades ago to monitor isolated variables rather than interpret complete industrial systems.

BNQTA seeks to reduce this gap by bringing accessible industrial intelligence to manufacturing operations.

The long-term vision is to democratize advanced industrial monitoring technologies that today remain economically inaccessible for many medium-sized companies.

Improving operational visibility ultimately enables higher productivity, better maintenance decisions, improved quality, lower operating costs and greater international competitiveness.

# 7. Looking Forward

BNQTA remains an active long-term technology initiative.

Current development focuses on creating an integrated platform capable of combining:

- Embedded sensing.
- Industrial IoT.
- Functional signal analysis.
- Predictive analytics.
- Artificial Intelligence.
- Manufacturing decision support.

Rather than replacing industrial automation systems, BNQTA aims to complement them by extracting operational knowledge that traditional monitoring systems do not capture.

---

# 8. Professional Significance

BNQTA should not be interpreted as a career change.

It represents the natural evolution of more than twenty years of industrial engineering experience.

The project integrates manufacturing knowledge, process engineering, operational analytics, systems thinking and technology into a unified architecture designed to solve the same industrial challenges from a new perspective.

In many ways, BNQTA is not separate from my previous career.

It is the technological continuation of it.

Looking back, I realize that every stage of my professional career contributed a piece of what eventually became BNQTA.

The manufacturing plants where I worked taught me how industrial systems behave.

The executive positions taught me how decisions are made.

The engineering projects taught me how to model complex problems.

Developing BNQTA taught me how to transform that accumulated experience into technology.
