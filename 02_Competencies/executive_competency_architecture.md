# 1. Purpose

This document defines the executive competency architecture that supports Alfredo Merejildo's professional positioning.

Rather than presenting isolated technical skills, competencies are organized into strategic capability domains that reflect the requirements of executive manufacturing leadership.

This framework serves as the primary source for ATS optimization, Executive CV generation, interview preparation and career positioning.

# 7. Operational Risk & Safety Engineering

## Purpose

Design safer, more reliable and sustainable industrial operations through systematic risk identification, engineering controls and organizational prevention strategies.

### Competencies
- IPERC (Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment and Control)
- Process Risk Assessment
- Incident Investigation
- Root Cause Analysis
- Ergonomic Improvement
- Emergency Planning
- Environmental Risk Reduction
- Engineering Controls
- Safe Work Systems
- Industrial Hygiene Fundamentals
- Operational Reliability
- Regulatory Compliance
- Safety Culture Development

### Demonstrated Applications
- Process redesign
- Pollution reduction
- Ergonomic improvements
- Selection of industrial tools
- PPE optimization
- Emergency response planning
- Accident investigation
- Preventive engineering
- Operational risk mitigation

### Executive Perspective

Safety is not treated as an independent compliance function.

It is integrated into operational design, engineering decisions and continuous improvement initiatives to build resilient manufacturing systems capable of sustaining long-term business performance.
