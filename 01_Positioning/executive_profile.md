# 1. Purpose

This document defines Alfredo Merejildo's executive positioning in the employment market.

Unlike a traditional professional summary, this document establishes the strategic narrative that will consistently guide every external representation of the candidate, including the Executive CV, LinkedIn profile, executive biography, interviews and cover letters.

The profile is designed to communicate executive capability, business impact and long-term leadership potential rather than simply describing previous positions.

---

# 2. Executive Positioning Statement

Alfredo Merejildo is a Manufacturing and Operations Executive with extensive experience leading industrial operations across multinational and national manufacturing companies in the plastics, food, metal-mechanical and industrial equipment sectors.

His professional career combines operational leadership, engineering expertise, financial discipline and continuous improvement methodologies to transform manufacturing systems into safer, more productive and financially sustainable operations.

More recently, this operational perspective has naturally evolved toward Industrial Digital Transformation, integrating Artificial Intelligence, Industrial IoT and advanced analytics as strategic enablers of operational excellence.

---

# 3. Executive Value Proposition

Alfredo creates business value by integrating four complementary executive capabilities:

* Operational Leadership:

Leading manufacturing operations focused on productivity, quality, safety and customer service.

* Business Improvement:

Transforming operational challenges into measurable business results through structured analysis, process optimization and financial discipline.

* Organizational Development:

Building autonomous, high-performance teams capable of sustaining continuous improvement initiatives.

* Industrial Digital Transformation:

Applying engineering knowledge together with emerging digital technologies to improve industrial decision-making and long-term operational competitiveness.

---

# 4. Executive Profile

Throughout more than fifteen years of progressive leadership, Alfredo has assumed increasing responsibilities in manufacturing, maintenance, industrial services and plant management.

His experience spans the complete operational value chain, including production planning, maintenance management, CAPEX execution, process engineering, operational excellence, cost optimization and people leadership.

Rather than specializing in a single functional discipline, he has developed the capability to understand manufacturing organizations as integrated business systems where operational, financial and human factors must evolve together.

This systemic perspective enables him to identify improvement opportunities that generate sustainable competitive advantages instead of isolated operational gains.

---

# 5. Leadership Identity

Alfredo's leadership philosophy is based on developing capable professionals rather than centralizing decision-making.

He believes that sustainable operational performance is achieved when leaders provide direction, remove barriers and create the conditions that allow technical specialists and supervisors to perform at their highest level.

His leadership style combines accountability, technical rigor, respect for people and organizational trust.

Operational discipline is expected without compromising professional development or collaborative problem solving.

---

# 6. Professional Differentiators

The following characteristics differentiate Alfredo from traditional manufacturing managers:

* Systems Thinking:

Industrial operations are approached as interconnected systems rather than isolated departments.

* Engineering-Based Decision Making:

Operational decisions are supported by structured analysis, technical evaluation and long-term sustainability.

* Business Perspective:

Engineering initiatives are evaluated according to their impact on productivity, operating costs, investment efficiency and organizational capability.

* Innovation with Purpose:

Technology is adopted only when it produces measurable operational improvements.

* Digital Manufacturing Vision:

Artificial Intelligence and Industrial IoT are viewed as natural evolutions of industrial engineering rather than independent technological disciplines.
